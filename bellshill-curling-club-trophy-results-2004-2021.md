# Bellshill Curling Club Trophy Results, 2004–2021

## Research notes

This document reconstructs the results of four Bellshill Curling Club competitions between 2004 and 2021 inclusive, using only archived snapshots of the club's own historical website via the Internet Archive Wayback Machine.

**Competitions researched**

1. **The Paterson Trophy** — a four-person team league (round-robin between skips' teams; the trophy is awarded on points, with shot difference used to break ties).
2. **The Bob Wilson Knock-Out Cup** — a four-person team single-elimination knock-out (usually 4–6 teams; final played in October–December, occasionally later).
3. **The McCall Salver** — a **two-person pairs** competition. Usually run as sectional round-robins followed by cross-over semi-finals and a final. Positions (Skip, Third, Second, Lead) are not applicable to this competition and have not been recorded.
4. **The Cleland Trophy** — a four-person team league (described on the archived scores index as "3rd's League"; format is round-robin between skips' teams).

**Archives consulted**

- Internet Archive Wayback Machine — `web.archive.org`
- Original site: `http://www.bellshillcurlingclub.com/` (2004–2005 pages under `/scores/`, `/fixtures/`, `/newsarchives.html`, `/news.html`, `/news0304.html`)
- Re-launched site: `http://www.bellshillcurlingclub.com/site/` (from 2006 onwards; pages under `/site/scores/`, `/site/fixtures/`, and the running `/site/news.html` narrative)
- Both `bellshillcurlingclub.com` and `bellshillcurlingclub.com/site` URL structures were investigated. The CDX API was queried for the whole domain and every unique URL that matched patterns of interest was enumerated.

**Year convention**

Bellshill's curling seasons run from autumn to spring. This document uses the convention that "year Y" corresponds to the season that **ended** in year Y (e.g. "2007" = the 2006-07 season, whose trophies were awarded at the Feb–Mar 2007 finals). This matches the year labels on archived scores pages (e.g. `paterson_scores_0607.htm` for the 2006-07 season).

**Limitations of the Wayback Machine**

The Wayback Machine's crawl of the club site was extremely sparse. In particular:

- The 2004-05 season was captured only in mid-season (Nov 2004 – Mar 2005), before finals were complete.
- The 2005-06 Cleland scores were not captured at all.
- Every season between 2007-08 and 2017-18 has fixtures/scores pages that were **listed in the archived scores index** but the linked pages themselves were **never captured** by the Wayback Machine. For those years the primary source is the running club news narrative (`site/news.html`) captured in Jan 2010, Nov 2010, Jan 2011, Sep 2016, Aug 2016 and May 2021.
- Between the March 2019 news snapshot and the May 2021 snapshot, the club stopped updating the website news (moved to Facebook), so nothing was posted online for the 2019-20 or 2020-21 season outcomes on the archived pages. The 2019-20 and 2020-21 scores pages were captured but with almost no data filled in.

**Status labels used**

| Label           | Meaning                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Found**       | Winner and runner-up established, with complete team/pair information for both.                                                                                  |
| **Partial**     | The result is established, but some player/team information is missing (e.g. one or more positions unlabelled in the source, or one team member not identified). |
| **Winner only** | The winner is established but the runner-up cannot be found.                                                                                                     |
| **Result only** | A competition result is known, but the winner/runner-up team information cannot be reliably established.                                                         |
| **Not found**   | No reliable result could be found in the available archived material. This does not mean the competition did not take place.                                     |
| **Unclear**     | Evidence exists but is contradictory or ambiguous.                                                                                                               |

**Key caveats**

- For four-person competitions, teams are recorded exactly as the source lists them. Where the archived page uses column headers `Skip | 3rd | 2nd | Lead` (e.g. the Bob Wilson KO and Cleland fixture pages), positions are treated as authoritative. Where the source only labels `(lead)` and `(skip)` (as is standard on the news page), the middle two players are recorded as "not specified in source" with their names shown in the order the source lists them (which by strong convention in curling news is Lead → Second → Third → Skip, but this convention has not been used to fill in positions).
- Names are preserved as spelled in the source (e.g. "Fiona Johnstone" and "Fiona Johnston" both occur; "Davie Armstrong" and "David Armstrong" both occur; "Anne Millar" and "Anne Miller" both occur; "Alistair Fergus" and "Alasdair Fergus" both occur).
- The 2004-05 (i.e. "2005") data on the archived pages is mid-season only. Every result recorded here for that year is drawn from the news of the _following_ season where possible; where no such news exists the result is recorded as "Not found".
- Substitutes have been preserved where the source explicitly mentions them (e.g. "Anne Hood (sub. for David Haggart)"). No attempt has been made to substitute players in from other years.

The August 2026 club update also referenced the Cleland Trophy result "Sarah Lean (lead), Joyce Cowie, John Adkin and David Buchanan (skip); runners-up Tom Russell (lead), Stewart Carson, Fiona Hetherington and Iain Buchanan (skip)." The archived 2019-20 Cleland team list (September 2019 snapshot) records only three teams — skipped by Iain Buchanan, Alison Bell and Alan Murdoch — with David Buchanan playing as a _lead_ in Alison Bell's team, not as a skip. On that basis this re-statement is taken as a confirmation of the 2018-19 result rather than as a separate 2019-20 result, and no 2019-20 Cleland entry has been added.
